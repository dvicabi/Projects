# Upstream asset notes

The original FeurStagram design assets and historical documentation are retained as reference material. Upstream font binaries are intentionally excluded from this publication package; the Android modification build does not depend on them. To reproduce the upstream design assets, obtain the fonts from their original upstream source and follow the retained SIL Open Font License notices.

Original design source: https://github.com/jean-voila/FeurStagram/tree/main/design
